How a text gets sent

1 · Trigger
A coordinator changes the Stage Advancer column in Monday (or a new item is created).
›
2 · Webhook
Monday calls the portal backend on a secret URL. Only the Stage Advancer column is watched.
›
3 · Gate
Backend sends only if the stage is patient-visible and tier 1 or 2. Tier 3 stays internal.
›
4 · Send
RingCentral SMS goes out, with the patient's personal portal link appended.
Every intake text ends with the same tracking line
The patient's private portal link is appended automatically to all Phase 0–3 messages: Track your progress: https://medicallymodern.com/portal?p={patient UID}  — the UID is a random identifier stored on the Monday item, so the link is unguessable and unique per patient. The Phase 4 subscriber text is the one exception; it carries its own bookmark link instead.

What to know before the demo

1 · Real patients are not being texted right now
The backend blocks every send unless the patient's name begins with [TEST]. Real patients are skipped with a logged reason until the PRODUCTION_SMS_ENABLED switch is turned on. This is the intended safety catch for development — but it does mean the system is currently silent for live patients, and flipping that switch is a deliberate go-live decision.
2 · The "don't nag the patient" timers are switched off
Two stages were designed to hold back: 1B should only text if 24 hours have passed, and 1D only after 3 days. That timing logic is currently bypassed in the code, so a text goes out on every advancement into those stages. If a coordinator moves a patient back and forth, the patient gets a duplicate text each time.
3 · The Insurance board has a stage the portal doesn't know about
The DVS stage exists in Monday but is missing from the portal's stage map. A patient parked there gets no text and their portal keeps showing whatever stage they were in previously, so the portal quietly goes stale rather than showing an error.
4 · "Scheduling Your Welcome Call" shows on the portal but sends nothing
Stage 3A is visible to the patient in the portal, but its message body is empty and it is classified as internal-tier, so no text is sent. The patient sees the stage change online without being notified.
5 · Dragging a patient into "Completed" does not send the final text
The 3C · You're All Set! text only fires when the Stage Advancer column is set to Completed. Moving the item into the Completed group by dragging it does nothing — no webhook is registered for group moves. Worth calling out to coordinators, since dragging is the more natural gesture.
6 · Coverage at a glance
Of the 19 stages, 8 intake stages text the patient and 11 stay silent. The silence is mostly deliberate — internal review steps and "stuck" states are hidden on purpose so patients aren't alarmed. The two unintentional gaps are DVS (note 3) and 3A (note 4).
